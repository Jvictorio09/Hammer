# 🎯 Hero Image Position - Slider Control Guide

## Modern Fine-Tune Adjustment!

You now have **TWO ways** to position your hero images:
1. **Quick Presets** - 9 buttons for common positions (fast!)
2. **Precision Sliders** - Drag to get the *exact* perfect view (smooth!)

---

## 🎨 How It Works

### Option 1: Quick Presets (Fast)
Click one of 9 arrows for instant positioning:

```
↖️  ⬆️  ↗️     Quick! Click for instant positioning
⬅️  ⭕  ➡️     
↙️  ⬇️  ↘️     
```

**Use when**: You want quick common positions (ceiling, floor, left, right, center)

---

### Option 2: Precision Sliders (Smooth & Exact!)

Below the buttons, you'll see **TWO SLIDERS**:

#### 🔄 Horizontal Slider (Left ← → Right)
```
|————————●————————|
Left   50%    Right
```
**Drag** to adjust horizontally:
- 0% = Far left
- 50% = Center (default)
- 100% = Far right

#### ⬍ Vertical Slider (Top ↑ ↓ Bottom)  
```
|————————●————————|
Top    50%   Bottom
```
**Drag** to adjust vertically:
- 0% = Top (ceiling, sky)
- 30% = Upper third (good for interiors!)
- 50% = Center (default)
- 70% = Lower third (furniture, ground)
- 100% = Bottom (floor)

---

## 💡 Real-Time Preview!

**As you drag the sliders**, the preview image above updates INSTANTLY so you can see exactly what will show on your page before saving!

---

## 🎯 Example: Fixing the Ceiling View

### Quick Way (Preset):
1. Click **⬇️ (down arrow)**
2. Preview updates → shows bottom
3. Save!

### Precise Way (Slider):
1. Find the **Vertical slider**
2. Drag it **to the left** (toward 30-40%)
3. Watch the preview update in real-time
4. Keep adjusting until you see the perfect view
5. Save when happy!

**Example Values**:
- Seeing ceiling? Try: **50% 30%** (shows upper-middle of room)
- Seeing floor only? Try: **50% 70%** (shows lower-middle)
- Perfect living room view: **50% 35%** (slightly above center)

## 🎨 Step-by-Step Tutorial

### Getting the Perfect View:

1. **Go to** `/dashboard/heroes/`
2. **Click "Edit"** on your hero
3. **Select your image** (Browse Gallery)
4. **Look at the preview** - seeing ceiling?
5. **Start with preset**: Click **⬇️** (rough adjustment)
6. **Fine-tune with slider**:
   - Grab the **Vertical slider**
   - Drag **left** (toward 30-40%) to see more of room
   - Watch preview update in real-time
   - Stop when you see the perfect view!
7. **Save Changes**
8. **Refresh page** - Perfect! 🎉

---

## 🎯 Pro Tips

### For Interior Photos:
- **Start with**: 50% 35% (slightly favors room over ceiling)
- **Adjust**: Drag vertical slider until furniture is centered
- **Sweet spot**: Usually between 30-40% for living rooms

### For Landscape Photos:
- **More sky**: 50% 20%
- **More ground**: 50% 70%
- **Balanced horizon**: 50% 50%

### For Product/People:
- **Face visible**: Adjust vertical to 20-40%
- **Full body**: Keep at 50%
- **Off-center subject**: Adjust horizontal slider
