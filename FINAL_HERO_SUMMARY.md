## 🎓 Step-by-Step Tutorial

### Complete Hero Setup (First Time):

1. **Login** as superuser → `/dashboard/`

2. **Click** "🎨 Page Heroes"

3. **Click "Edit"** on About hero

4. **Select Image**:
   - Click "Browse Gallery"
   - Click your image
   - URL auto-fills ✅

5. **Adjust Position**:
   - See preview (might show ceiling)
   - Click **⬇️** arrow
   - Preview updates to show lower part ✅

6. **Add Content** (if needed):
   - Headline: "Your amazing headline"
   - Subtext: "Supporting text here"

7. **Add Buttons** (optional):
   - Click "+ Add Button"
   - Text: "Get Started"
   - Link: "/contact/"
   - Style: Filled

8. **Add Pills** (optional):
   - Click "+ Add Pill"
   - Text: "20+ years"
   - Repeat for more

9. **Activate**:
   - Check ✅ "Active" checkbox

10. **Save Changes**

11. **Test**:
    - Visit `/about/`
    - See your perfect hero! 🎉
