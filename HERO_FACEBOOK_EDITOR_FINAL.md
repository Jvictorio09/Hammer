## 💻 Usage Guide

### Mouse Dragging:
1. **Hover** over the preview
2. **Click and hold**
3. **Drag UP** - Shows lower part of image (good for ceiling issue!)
4. **Drag DOWN** - Shows upper part
5. **Drag LEFT/RIGHT** - Horizontal adjustment
6. **Release** - Position updates
7. **Click "Apply"** when happy!

### Slider Control:
1. Use **Vertical slider**:
   - Drag **LEFT** (0-40%) = Shows lower part (furniture)
   - Drag **RIGHT** (60-100%) = Shows upper part (ceiling/sky)
2. Use **Horizontal slider**:
   - Drag for left/right adjustment
3. Position updates in real-time!

### Quick Presets:
- Click **⬇️** - Quick fix for ceiling (shows bottom)
- Click **⬆️** - Shows top
- Click **⭕** - Back to center
- Click any arrow for instant positioning!
