## 🎯 How to Use

### Step 1: Open Modal
- Click **"Adjust Position"** button
- Modal opens with your image

### Step 2: See What's Cropped
- **Dark/blurred areas** = Cropped out (ceiling, edges)
- **Clear area in teal frame** = What users see

### Step 3: Adjust Position
**Option A - Quick Presets:**
- Click ⬇️ → Shifts down (shows less ceiling)
- Watch dark areas change!

**Option B - Precise Sliders:**
- Drag **Vertical slider** left (to 35%)
- Watch image shift in real-time
- See ceiling move into dark (cropped) area
- See furniture move into clear (visible) area

### Step 4: Perfect!
- Keep adjusting until:
  - Furniture/subject in **clear area**
  - Ceiling/unwanted parts in **dark area**
- Click **"Apply Position"**
